# Visual & Audio Assets

- **Key Art & Logos** (`/src/assets/images/`): Custom generated high-contrast illustrations for Office Arena logo, Tank Arena banner/icon, and Target Sprint banner.
- **In-Game Art**: 100% crisp procedural Phaser 3 vector & geometry graphics (tanks, tracks, turrets, recoil barrels, striped steel barriers, breakable brick walls, crates, bushes, flags, shield rings, and pooled particle bursts) ensuring instant load times and zero broken textures offline.
- **Audio** (`TankArenaAudio.ts`): Zero-asset Web Audio API stereo-panned synthesizer generating tank shots, ricochet pings, crate breaks, shield pops, flag fanfare, countdown tones, and background music loops.
- **PWA Icons** (`/public/`): `icon.svg`, `apple-touch-icon.png`, `pwa-192x192.png`, `pwa-512x512.png`, and `pwa-maskable-512x512.png`.
