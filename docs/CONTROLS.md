# Control Styles & Custom PC Bindings

## PC (Keyboard & Mouse)
1. **Custom Keys & Mouse (`PC_CUSTOM`)**:
   - Bind any keyboard key (`Space`, `W`/`A`/`S`/`D`, Arrows, etc.) or mouse button (`Left Click`, `Right Click`, `Middle Click`) independently for **Drive Forward**, **Reverse**, **Turn Left**, **Turn Right**, and **Fire Shell**.
   - Includes one-click presets (`Space Move + Mouse Fire`, `Classic WASD + Mouse`, `Arrow Keys + Space`) and persists automatically across sessions in `localStorage`.
2. **Arcade (`PC_ARCADE`)**: Hold `Space` to drive forward, `Left Click` to fire + alternating recoil turn, `A`/`D` for subtle steering assist.
3. **Standard WASD (`PC_STANDARD` · Recommended)**: `W`/`S` forward & reverse, `A`/`D` rotate hull, `Space` or `Left Click` to fire cleanly without recoil turn.
4. **Mouse Steer (`PC_MOUSE_STEER`)**: Aim cursor directly to turn hull, hold `Right Click` or `W` to drive, `Left Click` or `Space` to fire.

## Mobile & Tablet (Touch)
1. **Twin-Thumb Stick (`STEER_TWIN_THUMB` · Recommended)**: Left floating thumb stick steers & drives (60% pivot threshold for sharp turns); right thumb taps/holds `FIRE`.
2. **Tank Pad (`TOUCH_TANK_PAD`)**: Explicit Left/Right turn buttons + Drive & Fire buttons.
3. **One-Thumb Arcade (`TOUCH_ONE_THUMB`)**: Hold anywhere to drive forward, quick tap to fire + recoil turn.

## Gamepad (`PAD_STEER`)
- Left Stick steers and throttles; `A` or `RT` fires; `Start` opens Pause menu.

## Local Party (2–4 Players Same Screen)
- **Keyboard**: P1 `Q`, P2 `P`, P3 `Z`, P4 `M`.
- **Touch**: 4 corner pads (hold to drive, tap to fire + recoil turn).
